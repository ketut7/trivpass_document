# Trivpass Visual Template Library

Five locked layout templates. **Do not add a sixth without analytics showing fatigue.** Strength comes from few templates used consistently, not from variety for its own sake.

All templates share the non-negotiables:
- Logo at the very top, **above the title** (jungle-green mark + lowercase serif `trivpass` + terracotta dot; reverse to off-white lettering on dark backgrounds).
- Warm off-white (#faf7f2) · jungle-green (#1f4d3a) · terracotta (#c2603e) only. No third color.
- Fraunces (serif) headlines, Inter (sans) body. Text flat: no outline, no shadow.
- **No faces.** People from behind / silhouette / hands OK. Objects and places preferred.
- No gradient, no drop shadow, no decorative chrome, no fake UI. Photos warm & slightly desaturated, never glossy/HDR.
- Output composed directly at 1080×1350 (4:5).

---

## Frequency ration (rotate, don't spread evenly)

| Template | Target share | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **T1** · Off-white top + photo bottom | ~40% | Workhorse — most covers & photo posts |
| **T3** · Text-led card | ~30% | Info / advice slides inside carousels |
| **T5** · Receipt / price card | ~15% | Whenever a price is shown |
| **T2** · Full-bleed photo + bottom panel | ~10% | Occasional strong-photo cover |
| **T4** · Jungle-green statement | **~5% — rarest** | Accent / hook. Max 1 per carousel or 1 per grid row. Like terracotta ≤2%: it only works when rare. |

---

## T1 — Off-white top + photo bottom (default)
Warm off-white upper area (logo → eyebrow → title → body), documentary photo fills the lower ~55%.
Use for: most covers, product spotlights, culture/real-Bali posts, photo slides.

## T2 — Full-bleed photo + bottom panel
Photo fills the entire frame; a solid off-white band across the lower third holds logo → eyebrow → title. Band is solid (no transparency, no gradient).
Use for: covers with a strong single image.

## T3 — Text-led card
Clean branded text card on warm off-white, no photo. Logo → eyebrow → title → body.
Use for: field guides, FAQ, trust steps, culture explainer text, any advice-dense slide.

## T4 — Jungle-green statement
Solid jungle-green (#1f4d3a) background, off-white text, reverse (off-white) wordmark with terracotta dot. One bold serif line + optional muted sub-line.
Use for: a single hook / quote / promise. **Ration hard.**

## T5 — Receipt / price card
Warm off-white background with a receipt-style card: itemized fee rows (label left, amount right, dotted leader), bold total row with the figure in Inter 600 terracotta. Prefixed "From" where range-based.
Use for: pricing / fee-breakdown posts (the Receipt-style content style).
**Never invent prices** — use real figures.

---

## Inner slides: photo or text-led?

Inner carousel slides are **not required to be text-led**. Default toward **T1 (text top + a relevant no-face photo)** when a genuinely relevant object/place image exists — it adds visual rhythm. Use **T3 (text-led)** for statements, definitions, checklists, or sacred/risky topics where a good photo isn't available or AI would garble it (e.g. religious objects). Mix within a carousel: don't make every inner slide text-led, and don't force a photo where it would be decorative filler.

## Product vs Education posts

Two visual modes — keep them distinct so the grid has rhythm and "bookable" content reads differently from editorial.

- **Education** (Real Bali, How Trivpass Works): calm, **text-led**. Lean T1/T3, photo secondary. Soft CTA (save / DM). Eyebrow → Fraunces title → body.
- **Product / offer** (Our Trips): **photo-forward + price-led**. The destination/attraction photo is the hero — use **T2** (full-bleed + bottom panel) or a large-photo T1. Required: product/tour **name** (Fraunces) + **price as a hero price block** (Inter 600, terracotta, "From IDR …"). Direct CTA ("Secure Your Tickets" / "Secure Your Driver & Tickets"). Optional: a **real ★ rating** (terracotta — never invented) and an optional driver add-on line.

**Discount — factual and understated only.** Acceptable: a small "was IDR X · now IDR Y" or a small "Save N%" tag. Never a loud OFF-sunburst, a struck-through hero price, "SALE/DISCOUNT" as the headline, or manufactured countdowns — that is OTA/discount-aggregator coding the brand rejects. Price is the hero; discount is a small honest note.

**Imagery:** a real person seen from behind looking at the real view is fine; **avoid staged influencer poses** (flowing dress, posed-for-camera) — Trivpass is "not an Instagram brand."

## Product templates (P1–P5)

Product / offer posts (Our Trips, Activities) use one of these **device** templates — photo-forward, price as hero. Match the device to the product. (T1–T5 remain the general layouts; P1–P4 are the product signatures.)

| Template | Device | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **P1 · The Pass** | A flat off-white matte ticket card (perforation + stub, hairline edge, no gloss) over a full-bleed photo; product name + hero price block; subtle monochrome QR / "ADMIT ONE" on the stub | Activities & Attractions (tickets) |
| **P2 · Hero Panel** | Full-bleed photo + solid off-white bottom panel; name + hero price block; optional real ★ rating | Tours / any product with a strong photo (max legibility) |
| **P3 · Tier Card** | Photo top ~40% + off-white lower area with Adult / Child / Family price rows + a small include/exclude line | Family attractions, multi-tier pricing |
| **P4 · Hero Overlay** | Full-bleed photo with the title overlaid (off-white Fraunces) on a clean/dark area + a small mini-pass price chip; NO panel. Use only when the photo has a clean dark area for text | Immersive hero shots |
| **P5 · Receipt Hero** | A receipt card with a perforated / wavy top edge overlapping a full-bleed photo; rows **mirror the live booking** (N adults × price = subtotal, then inclusions marked "Included"), with a hero Total price block | Tours / fee transparency (driver-included products) |

All P-templates: **price is the hero** (Inter 600 terracotta); direct CTA ("Secure Your Tickets" / "Secure Your Driver & Tickets"); **no faces** (person from behind only); discount **factual & understated** (no sunburst / struck-through hero); flat brand palette, no gloss/chrome. The pass / panel / chip is the only graphic device. See "Product vs Education posts" above.

### P5 · Receipt Hero — prompt skeleton

Rows mirror the live booking (a "N adults × price" line that totals, then inclusions marked "Included"). Never invent per-line amounts; add-ons (bigger vehicle, far-out pickup) appear as "+ IDR …" only when selected.

```
Create a 4:5 Instagram product post (portrait, 1080x1350). Trivpass brand — Template P5 (Receipt Hero).

PHOTO: full-bleed across the top — <destination at golden hour>, warm light, no people (or a tiny distant figure from behind).

RECEIPT CARD: a flat warm off-white (#faf7f2) card with a perforated / wavy top edge, overlapping the lower ~55% of the frame (hairline edge, NO gloss, NO drop shadow):
- Trivpass logo (jungle-green ticket mark + lowercase serif "trivpass" #1f4d3a + terracotta dot), top-left.
- Eyebrow, uppercase terracotta (#c2603e): "WHAT'S IN THE PRICE"
- Title, warm serif (Fraunces), jungle-green (#1f4d3a): "<product name>"
- (optional) Sub-line, muted-gray (#6a706c): "<full day · duration · pickup zone>"
- Transaction rows (Inter), label left in #3a3f3c, value right-aligned, joined by a thin dotted leader:
    <N> adults × IDR <price> ............... IDR <subtotal>     (value in ink #1a1d1b)
    Vehicle: <MPV> ........................ Included            (value jungle-green #1f4d3a, medium)
    <other inclusions, comma-joined> ...... Included            (value jungle-green, medium)
    <selected add-on, if any> ............. + IDR <amount>
- Total row: "Total — IDR <total>", figure in Inter 600 terracotta (#c2603e).

STYLE (Trivpass brand, strict): photorealistic warm documentary photo; receipt is flat matte paper, brand palette only. NO faces, NO gloss, NO gradient, NO drop shadow, NO neon, NO third color, NO decorative graphics. Text flat, no outline.
RULE: rows mirror the live booking — inclusions say "Included", never invented per-line amounts. Use real figures.
```

## Grid rhythm
Think in a 3×3 grid: alternate photo-led (T1/T2) and text-led (T3/T5); drop in at most one T4 statement per row. Varied but unmistakably Trivpass.
