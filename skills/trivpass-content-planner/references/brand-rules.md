# Trivpass Brand Rules

## Positioning

Trivpass is a Bali-based travel agency operated from Seminyak. It is not a marketplace, OTA reseller, hotel concierge, discount aggregator, or chat-group driver booker.

Core line: "Real drivers. Real prices. No surprises."

Primary promises:

- Verified drivers contracted directly by Trivpass.
- Transparent all-in pricing.
- 24/7 ops in Seminyak.
- Cultural access handled carefully and respectfully when ready.

## Current Product Status

Custom Trips are not released. Do not promote AI trip planning or custom-trip builder.

Cultural Events are preparing unless the user or website says otherwise. Use cultural content for education, etiquette, and calendar literacy. Do not imply live booking, confirmed village agreements, or guaranteed ceremony access.

## Voice

Sound calm, specific, transparent, and useful. Lead with what the traveler needs to know.

Do:

- Use specific places, times, inclusions, route logic, and constraints.
- Explain what is included and what is not.
- Answer anxieties: scams, overcharging, failed booking, route confusion, cultural uncertainty.
- Use "Activities & Attractions" as the umbrella term.

Avoid:

- "Book now" in post copy.
- "Authentic", "hidden gem", "magical", "breathtaking", "premium", "luxury", "world-class", "hassle-free", "once-in-a-lifetime".
- "Cheapest in Bali", "#1 in Bali", "exclusive", "banjar approved", "village endorsed", or "Pemangku certified".
- Overstating cultural access before agreements exist.
- "No reseller markup" or "no markup" for resold tickets — Trivpass marks up from the Nett (cost) price, so this is false. Use "the price you see is the price you pay / no hidden fees" instead. ("No commission stops" is fine — that is the driver model, not ticket markup.)
- Showing the Nett (cost) price as the public "From" price, or a misleading "from" using the cheapest tier (e.g. child / domestic) for an international audience.

## Content Pillars

- Bali Travel Education: tips, etiquette, timing, route planning, weather, transport.
- Trust Building: driver vetting, no commission stops, transparent pricing, refund windows, Seminyak ops.
- Brand Introduction: what Trivpass is and is not.
- Tour Packages: practical itinerary and route content.
- Activities & Attractions: tickets, family days, water activities, wildlife, shows, adventure, wellness.
- Cultural Literacy: Galungan, Kuningan, PKB, temple etiquette, odalan, purnama, canang sari, visitor expectations.
