## The 30-second pitch

**Trivpass is an Indonesian travel agency operating from Seminyak, Bali.** We run one internal team, one driver roster we vet ourselves, and one curated calendar of real Bali cultural events. Travelers book verified drivers and authentic experiences at transparent, all-in prices — without the commission layer, the chat-group dispatch, or the staged tourist shows.

We are *not* a marketplace, an OTA reseller, or a Telegram-driver broker. The thing we sell is the absence of the middleman.

---

## What "on-brand" means in one paragraph

Trivpass is the **calm anti-OTA**. Our voice answers the traveler's three real anxieties — *will I get scammed*, *am I being overcharged*, *will the booking actually work* — before it talks about culture or ethics. Our pricing is itemized down to the parking fee. Our drivers are named, photographed, and contracted directly by us — vetted on documents, vehicle, and language before they take guests. Our cultural events are real ceremonies attended as respectful guests of the host village, never reenactments. When we describe ourselves, we lead with safety and price clarity; the cultural-respect story is the backbone, not the headline.

## Brand decisions you will be asked about — and the answer

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Are we a "platform"? | **No.** We are an agency. The word "platform" is banned in copy. |
| Are we a "marketplace"? | **No.** There are no third-party operators we collect a fee from. |
| Do we say "Book now"? | **No.** The canonical CTA is **"Secure Your Driver & Tickets"**. |
| What is the brand color? | **Jungle Green** (`#1f4d3a`) with a **Terracotta** (`#c2603e`) accent — the v1 production palette. |
| Do we expose the founders publicly? | **No.** The trust story leans on policies, voice, photography, and the Seminyak address — not on personal credibility. |
| Do we claim "village endorsed" or "banjar approved"? | **No.** Those overclaim partnerships we don't have on paper yet. |
| Do we use stock photography? | **Never.** Every image is real — the location, the driver, the ceremony. |
| Do we display prices in USD? | Display only — we always charge in IDR. Display values are snapshotted at confirm time. |
