# 02 · Positioning

> Where Trivpass sits in the Bali travel market — and why none of the existing options solve the same problem.

---

## The competitive landscape (and where we fit)

| Player | What they actually are | Where they fall short |
|---|---|---|
| **Klook / GetYourGuide / Viator** | OTA marketplaces. They list third-party tours and take a commission. | Their economics depend on broker margin and SKU count. They cannot vet thousands of operators, and they have no opinion on the experience itself. |
| **Hotel concierge** | A front-desk handoff to whichever vendor pays the hotel a commission. | The concierge is constrained to the hotel's commission deals. No real ceremony access; no driver continuity. |
| **Telegram / WhatsApp driver** | A freelancer who advertises in expat chat groups. | No vetting, no insurance, no support, no curation. When something goes wrong, there's no one to call. |
| **Luxury concierge ($1000s/day)** | Bespoke trip designers serving high-net-worth travelers. | Great product, wrong price point. Not a service for normal couples on a 7–10 day Bali trip. |
| **DIY (Google Maps + Grab + Booking.com)** | The traveler stitches it themselves. | Works for the streetwise solo traveler; falls apart for couples, families, and first-timers. Tourist scams target this segment. |
| **Trivpass** | A vertically-integrated travel agency. Internal driver roster, internal dispatch, internal ops. | We have to build the roster and the cultural-partner network ourselves — there's no shortcut. That's also the moat. |

---

## The positioning statement

> **For travelers who want the real Bali without the scams, the haggling, or the staged tourist shows — Trivpass is the locally-operated travel agency that handles every part of the trip in-house, at a transparent all-in price.**

Components of that statement, broken down:

- **"For travelers who want the real Bali"** — the audience knows the Instagram version is performative and wants the actual island. They're not looking for an Instagram swing in Ubud.
- **"without the scams, the haggling, or the staged tourist shows"** — names the three anxieties directly. We lead with what we remove, not what we add.
- **"locally-operated travel agency"** — anchors that we are an agency, not a platform, and that we are run from Bali, not Singapore.
- **"handles every part of the trip in-house"** — the vertical-integration claim. Drivers, ops, support all under one roof.
- **"at a transparent all-in price"** — the price-clarity promise. The number you see is the number you pay.

---

## What makes the model defensible

The moat is **not software**. Anyone with engineers can build a booking site in a quarter. The moat is the three things that take years to build:

1. **The internal driver roster.** Drivers we vet, train, and contract directly. Each one onboarded over 4–6 weeks with documented vetting, vehicle inspection, language assessment, and a supervised probation period. A competitor would need to recruit, train, contract, and operate every driver from Bali to match.
2. **The cultural-partner network.** Written agreements with host *banjars* (village councils) and *Pemangku* (priests) — covering visitor conduct, group-size limits, sacred-date cancellation rights, and a per-traveler community contribution. This is relationship work that no acquisition can shortcut.
3. **The brand promise of "no commission stops."** The economic discipline of paying drivers enough to never need a side-deal. Telegram drivers and OTA-listed freelancers can't match this without changing their business model.

None of those three are software problems. All three would take a competitor 18+ months to replicate.

---

## What we are *not* trying to be

It is just as important to name what we explicitly avoid becoming:

- **Not a global travel brand.** Trivpass is for Bali. Expansion to other Indonesian destinations is a "maybe in three years" question — not a 2026 question.
- **Not a venture-scale platform.** The unit economics work as an agency with salaries and trip margins. We are not optimizing for marketplace take-rate.
- **Not an Instagram brand.** We don't manufacture content. The photography is real, the ceremonies are real, the drivers are real. If we wouldn't put it on the website with names attached, we don't put it on Instagram.
- **Not a discount aggregator.** We do not undercut on price; we are transparent on price. There is no "sale" pricing because there are no hidden margins to give back.

## The "why now" story

Three things make 2026 the right year to build Trivpass:

1. **Bali tourism is back to pre-pandemic volumes** — and the broker model is more saturated than ever. The market is begging for an alternative.
2. **Indonesian payment infrastructure has matured** — Stripe + Xendit cover card, bank transfer, and e-wallets in a single integration. Five years ago this required an in-country payment partnership.
3. **AI is good enough to replace the bottom layer of trip-planning** — our custom-trip builder produces drafts that experienced travelers actually use as a starting point. We don't have to hire a 20-person trip-design team to scale the personalization.

The agency model would have worked in 2018; the technology to scale it without growing headcount linearly is what's new.

## How to talk about competitors

A house-rule: **we never disparage by name.** Klook, Viator, GetYourGuide are real companies serving real travelers — they just solve a different problem. Use phrasing like:

- *"Marketplaces are good for finding the cheapest tour. We're built for travelers who care more about whether the trip works than whether it's the cheapest option."*
- *"Hotel concierges are constrained by their vendor relationships. We're a standalone agency, so we can recommend the trip you actually want, not the trip the hotel gets a kickback on."*
- *"Telegram drivers are great if you happen to know someone. Most travelers don't. We exist to make 'knowing someone' the default, not the exception."*

Never: *"Klook sucks"*, *"OTAs are scams"*, *"Don't use a hotel concierge"*. Those are losing framings.
