Three pillars. Every post belongs to one of them.

| Pillar | Purpose | Post ratio |
|---|---|---|
| **Pillar 1 · Real Bali** | Educate travelers about Bali culture, ceremonies, traditions, and what the island is actually like. Builds brand authority. | 40% |
| **Pillar 2 · Our Trips** | Showcase tour packages, activities & attractions. Transparent pricing, real itineraries. Converts browsers into bookers. | 35% |
| **Pillar 3 · How Trivpass Works** | Trust-building. Driver vetting, all-in pricing, 24/7 ops. Answers the three traveler anxieties. | 25% |

## 3. Voice on Social Media

Same brand voice — adapted slightly for social.

- **No exclamation marks.** Confidence, not excitement.
- **Max 1 emoji per post.** Only in caption, never in headline text on the image.
- **Lead with a fact or a question**, not with "We're excited to…"
- **Always end with a CTA** — soft on educational posts, direct on product posts.
- **No banned words:** authentic, hidden gem, once-in-a-lifetime, magical, stunning, breathtaking, hassle-free, world-class.

**Caption structure:**
```
[Hook — 1 sentence. Fact, question, or bold statement.]

[Body — 2–4 short sentences. Specific, numbered where possible.]

[CTA — 1 line. Soft or direct depending on pillar.]

[Hashtags — 8–12, on a separate line or in first comment]
```

## 7. Photo & Creative Brief Guide

Trivpass should use real photography wherever a place, driver, vehicle, ceremony, or trip is being represented. No stock photography. No AI-generated Bali scenery. No re-staged ceremonies.

If real photography is not available yet, use a branded text-led graphic on warm off-white with the mark or wordmark. Do not invent a location, driver, ceremony, or product visual.

### By content type:

**Bali rice terraces (Tegalalang):**
- Real early-morning terrace photo, preferably before crowds.
- Show the walking path, light, and terrain honestly.
- Avoid over-saturated greens and heavy HDR edits.

**Temple routes — Lempuyang, Tirta Empul, Uluwatu, Tanah Lot:**
- Use real route photography from the actual itinerary.
- Keep tourists, queues, and weather believable when relevant.
- Do not remove crowds if queue timing is part of the service promise.

**Mt. Batur Sunrise:**
- Capture the real pickup-to-summit sequence: dark road, trail, summit, breakfast, descent.
- A short Reel is stronger than a single heroic image.

**Ceremonies and offerings:**
- Photograph only with permission and cultural guidance.
- Frame travelers as respectful guests, not as the center of the ceremony.
- Do not market cultural events as live until written agreements exist.

**Driver trust/verification:**
- Real driver portrait beside the actual vehicle.
- Clear face, plate/model detail when appropriate, neat but not glossy.
- Add the circular verified mark only in designed layouts, not as if it exists in the real scene.

**Uluwatu + Kecak + Jimbaran:**
- Build a sequence: cliff temple around 16:00, Kecak at sunset, dinner after.
- This route works well as a 3-slide carousel or 15-30 second Reel.

**PKB Arts Festival:**
- Use official or Trivpass-shot event photography only where usage rights are clear.
- Credit the festival/source if required.

**Branded post background:**
- Warm off-white paper texture, Jungle Green type, Terracotta signal elements.
- Best for fee breakdowns, refund windows, driver vetting steps, and FAQ posts.

## 9. Paid Ads Roadmap (Meta Ads)

Start organic for the first 4 weeks. Begin paid in July once you have 8–12 solid posts to point to.

### Phase 1 — Page Likes Campaign (July, Week 1)
- **Goal:** Build audience before spending on conversion
- **Format:** Single image or short video
- **Audience:** 
  - Location: Australia, UK, Canada, USA
  - Age: 28–45
  - Interest: Bali, travel, Indonesia, cultural travel
- **Budget:** Start at USD 5–10/day
- **Creative:** Brand intro post ("Real drivers. Real prices. No surprises.")

### Phase 2 — Awareness / Reach Campaign (July, Week 2–3)
- **Goal:** Reach travelers planning a Bali trip
- **Format:** Carousel (tour packages with itemized pricing)
- **Audience:** Add behavioral layer — "Frequent international travelers", "Engaged shoppers"
- **Budget:** USD 10–15/day
- **Creative:** Tour package carousels with real itinerary + all-in price

### Phase 3 — Conversion Campaign (August onward)
- **Goal:** Drive bookings to trivpass.com
- **Format:** Single image + strong CTA
- **Pixel:** Install Meta Pixel on trivpass.com before launching this phase
- **Retargeting:** Anyone who visited the site but didn't book
- **Budget:** USD 20–30/day
- **Creative:** "Mt. Batur Sunrise Hike — from IDR 1,200,000 per traveler. Driver + tickets + breakfast on the volcano. All in." + "Secure Your Driver & Tickets" button

## 10. Monthly Review Checklist

At the end of each month, check:

- [ ] Which post had the highest reach? Which had the most saves? (Saves = content worth repeating)
- [ ] Which pillar got the most engagement? Adjust the 40/35/25 ratio accordingly.
- [ ] Did any caption generate DMs or comments asking about booking? That's a lead — respond within 2 hours.
- [ ] Are follower demographics matching the target audience (AU/UK/CA/US, 28–45)?
- [ ] Is the Reel performing better than feed posts? (If yes, increase Reel frequency.)
- [ ] Review ad spend vs. link clicks. CPL (cost per link click) target: under USD 0.80.
