# Trivpass Partner X-Banner

## Files

- `trivpass-partner-xbanner.svg` — draft visual, 80 x 180 cm.

## Placement

Use this banner for villa, restaurant, cafe, and front-desk partner placements where the guest is already in Bali and deciding what to do next.

## Core Message

Headline:

> Real drivers. Real prices. No surprises.

Supporting copy:

> Plan tomorrow's Bali day with verified drivers, all-in prices, and 24/7 Seminyak ops.

CTA:

> Scan to secure your driver & tickets

Do not use "Book Now".

## Why This Direction Fits Trivpass

- Leads with traveler anxiety: driver trust, price clarity, and booking reliability.
- Uses the brand's receipt logic as a trust signal.
- Avoids stock-photo travel fantasy while real Trivpass photography is unavailable.
- Keeps the palette limited to warm off-white, jungle green, and terracotta.
- Works in a partner setting because the copy is fast to understand from a few meters away.

## Print Specs

- Final size: 80 x 180 cm.
- Export target: PDF/X-1a or PDF/X-4, CMYK.
- Resolution for any added photography: 300 DPI at final size.
- Recommended safe margin: at least 50 mm from all edges.
- Keep QR code at least 120 x 120 mm in the final print.
- Keep critical text and QR clear of bottom hardware/clamp area.

## QR And Tracking

Replace the placeholder QR with a tracked URL before print.

Suggested UTM:

```text
https://trivpass.com/?utm_source=partner&utm_medium=xbanner&utm_campaign=villa_restaurant&utm_content={partner_slug}
```

For each partner, use a unique `utm_content` or partner code.

Examples:

- `utm_content=villa_ayana_lobby`
- `utm_content=restaurant_seminyak_counter`
- `utm_content=villa_canggu_room_a`

## Optional Variants

Villa version:

> Tomorrow's driver should not be a guessing game.

Restaurant version:

> Know where you are going after lunch.

Front desk version:

> Driver, tickets, and trip costs in one clear checkout.

Keep the proof points the same unless the placement has a specific audience.

## Production Notes

- Convert text to outlines before sending to print if the vendor does not have Fraunces and Inter.
- Replace the QR placeholder with a real QR code.
- Add real Trivpass or destination photography only if the image is from the actual place/product and cleared for use.
- Do not add gradients, glossy effects, heavy shadows, influencer poses, or stock travel imagery.
