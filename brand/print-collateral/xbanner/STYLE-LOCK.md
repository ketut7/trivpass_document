## Layout

Use a vertical 80 x 180 cm x-banner composition.

1. Top hero photo section with logo tab and primary message.
2. Middle off-white product section with the headline and 3 image tiles.
3. Large QR CTA card.
4. Bottom text-only proof strip with 01 / 02 / 03.

Do not add the old bottom category icon row. The categories should appear only once as image tiles.

## Brand Notes

- Use warm off-white, jungle green, and sparse terracotta accents.
- Keep a matte editorial travel-collateral feel.
- Use real/cleared photography for final print.
- Pura/temple imagery is acceptable as tour context, but avoid implying ceremony access.
- Replace the QR placeholder with a real tracked partner QR before print.
- Rebuild final text in vector/SVG/PDF for print sharpness.
