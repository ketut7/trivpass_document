# 03 · Value Proposition — The Four Pillars

> Every value statement Trivpass makes ladders up to one of these four pillars. If a claim doesn't fit one of them, it doesn't go on the website.

---

## The four pillars at a glance

| Pillar | What it means | Why travelers care |
|---|---|---|
| **One internal driver roster** | Every driver is contracted directly with Trivpass — no broker, no chat-group dispatch, no gig-app pickup | No commission stops, no surprise side-deals |
| **Real cultural events** | Ceremonies travelers join as invited guests of the host *banjar* — not staged tourist shows | Authenticity, dignity, cultural respect |

## Pillar 1 — One internal driver roster

### The claim

**Every Trivpass driver is an internal contractor we vet, train, and dispatch ourselves. Not a freelancer. Not a chat-group dispatch. Not an OTA-listed operator. Not a gig-app pickup.**

### What it means operationally

- **Contracted directly with Trivpass, not via a broker or marketplace.** This is the load-bearing structural choice — it's what lets us hold drivers to a single set of standards and a single anti-scam policy.
- **Background checks, vehicle inspections, language tests** before a driver joins the roster.
- **4–6 week onboarding** — document review (SIM, KTP, SKCK police clearance), 38-point vehicle inspection, English road test, code-of-conduct training, supervised probation period. (Full detail in [06-trust-and-safety.md](06-trust-and-safety.md).)
- **Performance bar:** rolling rating threshold; drivers below it go into formal review. A single confirmed scam report = permanent off-board.
- **90-day vehicle re-inspections, regular conduct reviews, 24-month police-clearance refresh.**

### Why this matters to the traveler

This is the answer to *"will I get scammed?"* — the most common, least talked-about anxiety travelers carry into Bali. When the driver works directly with Trivpass — not for a broker who skims, not for shops that pay him to detour you — the entire shape of the trip changes. No silver-shop stop pretending to be "on the way." No coffee-plantation lunch that you didn't ask for. No "let me introduce you to my cousin who has a deal." Just the trip you booked.

### What we can say

- *"All drivers are internal contractors — not random gig-app pickups."*
- *"Background checks, vehicle inspections, language tests."*
- *"Every driver works directly with Trivpass — no broker layer, no Telegram chat groups."*
- *"The day before pickup, we email you your driver's name, contact, and license plate. No surprises at the curb."*
- *"Every driver completes a documented 4–6 week onboarding before joining the roster."*

### What we cannot say

- ❌ *"The best drivers in Bali."* — unprovable, generic.
- ❌ *"All drivers speak fluent English."* — they meet a conversational road-test bar, not fluency.
- ❌ *"Salaried drivers"* / *"Salaried roster"* — we deliberately moved away from the salary framing in current copy. Use *"internal contractors"* or *"drivers we vet and contract directly"* instead.
- ❌ *"Family-owned operators."* — most drivers are individuals contracted directly with Trivpass, not family operators.

## Pillar 2 — Real cultural events

### The claim

**Travelers attend real Bali ceremonies as invited, respectful guests of the host village — never as audience members at a staged show.**

### What it means operationally

- **The cultural calendar follows the Bali Hindu calendar.** Galungan and Kuningan (the 210-day Pawukon cycle), Nyepi with its eve-of Ogoh-Ogoh parade, Melasti pre-Nyepi sea purification, Omed-Omedan day-after-Nyepi, village-specific *odalan* (including Odalan Besakih at the mother temple), Mekotek on every Kuningan, and the annual Pesta Kesenian Bali (PKB) arts festival. Sacred dates drive availability; we never schedule a ceremony for tourist convenience.
- **Group-size caps** — typically 12 visitors per event. Some are capped at 8. This is set by the host *banjar*, not by us.
- **Community contribution** — IDR 100,000 per traveler goes directly to the host village. Displayed transparently on every cultural-event card.
- **Cultural-respect briefing** before every event — sarong + sash provided, photography rules explained, ritual sequence walked through.
- **Sacred-date cancellation rights** — if the *banjar* cancels (births, deaths, ritual conflicts), travelers get a full refund automatically.

### Why this matters to the traveler

This is the answer to *"is what I'm doing in Bali actually Bali?"* — a quieter anxiety, more pronounced in older travelers and repeat visitors. Most Bali "cultural experiences" on the market are reenacted for tourists or held at venues designed for spectator photography. Trivpass cultural events are the ones the village is doing anyway; you are an invited guest. You leave with the experience, the village leaves with the community contribution, and nothing about the ceremony is changed for your presence.

### Important: status of this product line

In v1 (current state), **cultural events are aspirational**. The partner network and curated calendar are still being built. The public site treats this product line as "being curated" — we do not list events we do not yet have written agreements for. Do not market the cultural-events line as fully launched until the agreements exist.

### What we can say (once launched)

- *"Real ceremonies, attended as guests of the host village."*
- *"IDR 100,000 of every booking goes directly to the community hosting the ceremony."*
- *"Typically capped at 12 visitors — set by the host *banjar*."*
- *"If the *banjar* cancels for a sacred reason, you get a full refund. No re-staging."*

### What we cannot say

- ❌ *"Banjar approved" / "Village endorsed" / "Pemangku certified"* — these overclaim formal endorsements we do not have. (Even if we have a written agreement, the *banjar* is hosting us as guests; they are not certifying us.)
- ❌ *"Authentic Bali"* — overused, meaningless. Show the authenticity through photography and detail, don't claim it as an adjective.
- ❌ *"Exclusive"* — the events are not exclusive to Trivpass; the village runs them regardless of us.

## Pillar 3 — Transparent pricing

### The claim

**The number you see at booking is the number you pay. Driver, tickets, fuel, parking, taxes, water — all in. No checkout surprises. No haggling at the gate.**

### What it means operationally

- **All-in pricing.** Every booking displays the breakdown: driver fee, activity / attraction tickets, fuel, parking, taxes, community contribution (if applicable), service fee. No category is hidden.
- **The receipt-style reservation card** is the layout signature of the brand — itemized fees displayed as dot-leader rows (`DRIVER FEE..........IDR 450,000`) so the user sees the working. (See [08-visual-identity.md](08-visual-identity.md).)
- **IDR is the charge currency** always. Display can be in USD/AUD/EUR/etc., but the conversion is shown live and the IDR figure is what hits the card.
- **No "service fee" surprise at checkout.** There is no platform fee, no booking fee, no handling fee. The price on the listing page is the price at confirm.
- **Refund windows are publicly stated** — 100% > 48h, 50% 24–48h, 0% <24h, plus 100% on driver no-show or ceremony cancellation. No fine print.

### Why this matters to the traveler

This is the answer to *"am I being overcharged?"* — the anxiety that drives the most pre-booking shopping behavior. Travelers compare three OTA prices before they book, because they assume one is hiding a fee. Trivpass removes that work: the price is the price. The transparency promise is encoded in the layout of the booking page, not just in the copy.

### What we can say

- *"Every fee is itemized. No checkout surprises."*
- *"The number you see is the number you pay."*
- *"All-in: driver, tickets, fuel, parking, taxes, water — included."*
- *"Refund windows are public. Read them before you book."*

### What we cannot say

- ❌ *"Cheapest in Bali."* — we are not optimizing for cheapest; we are optimizing for honest.
- ❌ *"No fees."* — there are fees; they are itemized. The discipline is transparency, not absence.
- ❌ *"Best value."* — generic. Replace with a specific, itemized comparison.

## Pillar 4 — 24/7 ops in Seminyak

### The claim

**When something goes wrong on your trip, you call one phone number into our Seminyak office. Real Trivpass staff, not an outsourced call center. One team owns the resolution.**

### What it means operationally

- **24/7 phone line into the Seminyak office.** Single number, English/Indonesian/Bahasa, no menu tree.
- **Driver phone, guide phone, ops phone** — three contact paths during a trip. The driver intro email (sent the night before) carries all of them.
- **GPS tracking on every vehicle.** Ops sees where the driver is in real time.
- **Backup driver dispatch within 15 minutes** if a driver doesn't show. Refund issued automatically.
- **Email channels:** `hello@`, `trips@`, `safety@` — separated by urgency, all owned by the same Seminyak team.

### Why this matters to the traveler

This is the answer to *"will this booking actually work?"* — the anxiety that surfaces *after* the booking is paid. Online travel is full of confirmation emails that go to ghost inboxes when the day arrives. Trivpass exists to be the team that picks up the phone at 6:30am when the ferry is cancelled.

### What we can say

- *"Real people in our Seminyak office, 24 hours a day. Not an outsourced call center."*
- *"If a driver doesn't show within 15 minutes, we send a backup and refund the trip."*
- *"Three contact paths every trip: driver, guide, ops."*

### What we cannot say

- ❌ *"Instant response."* — we don't guarantee response time; we guarantee a human picks up.
- ❌ *"Worldwide support."* — we operate in Bali. Don't imply otherwise.

## How to choose which pillar to lead with

| Context | Lead pillar |
|---|---|
| Homepage hero | Driver roster (the anti-scam framing) |
| Cultural-event listing | Real cultural events |
| Activity- or attraction-detail price block | Transparent pricing |
| Confirmation email / post-booking | 24/7 ops |
| Investor / partnership deck | Driver roster, with the moat argument |
| Press release | Real cultural events (most distinctive) |
| Recovering from an incident | 24/7 ops, with empathy first |
