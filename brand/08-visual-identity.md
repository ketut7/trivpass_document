# 08 · Visual Identity

> A summary of the visual system, sized for a CEO / non-designer to brief out work. The full design tokens live in [`resources/css/app.css`](../../resources/css/app.css); this file is the strategic version.

---

## The visual identity in one sentence

**Trivpass looks like the travel agency you wish you had a friend at: warm off-white paper, jungle-green ink, terracotta accents, a Fraunces-serif wordmark, and real photography of the actual places you'd go.**

---

## The brand metaphor

Where most travel brands reach for one of two visuals — *editorial luxury magazine* or *Instagram-glossy app* — Trivpass reaches for something quieter. **A trusted local agency office.** A warm interior, brass-and-paper signage, a notebook on the desk, real photographs on the wall of the places they actually send you to. Calm, deliberate, unglamorous, confident.

Every visual decision can be checked against this metaphor:

- Does this surface feel like the inside of a trusted office? (Yes / no.)
- Does the price feel like it's written by hand on a card, or generated by a marketplace template? (Yes / no.)
- Does the typography read as something written by someone who knows Bali, not by a brand template? (Yes / no.)

If the answer is no, the design has drifted.

## Colors

These are the **production design tokens** from [`resources/css/app.css`](../../resources/css/app.css). They are the only acceptable values. Hex appears in that one file; everywhere else reads `var(--color-*)`.

### Brand

| Token | Name | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--color-primary` | **Jungle Green** | `#1f4d3a` | Wordmark, primary CTA fills, link emphasis, "verified" / "confirmed" status |
| `--color-primary-hover` | | `#2a6149` | Pointer-hover variant |
| `--color-primary-pressed` | | `#163a2c` | Press / active variant |
| `--color-primary-soft` | | `#e8efe9` | Verified-driver chips, refund-guarantee pills, status tints |
| `--color-primary-disabled` | | `#b9cdc1` | Disabled CTAs |
| `--color-on-primary` | | `#faf7f2` | Off-white text on jungle-green fills |

Jungle Green is the brand. It carries trust, action, and identity all at once. **No teal, no navy, no near-black is acceptable as a substitute.**

### Accent

| Token | Name | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--color-accent` | **Terracotta** | `#c2603e` | Prices, scarcity badges, star ratings, the wordmark's accent dot, eyebrow labels — **signal, never action** |
| `--color-accent-hover` | | `#d27651` | Hover on terracotta links |
| `--color-accent-soft` | | `#f7e8e0` | Limited-availability cards, "exclusive cultural access" callouts |
| `--color-on-accent` | | `#faf7f2` | Off-white text on terracotta surfaces |

**Terracotta is not a CTA color.** It's used to *signal* — to draw the eye to a price or a scarcity message. CTAs are always Jungle Green fills.

### Canvas

| Token | Name | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--color-canvas` | **Warm Off-White** | `#faf7f2` | Default page background |
| `--color-surface-1` | | `#f3efe8` | Filter bands, FAQ accordions |
| `--color-surface-2` | | `#ede8de` | Hovered cards, featured cells |
| `--color-surface-3` | | `#e6e1d4` | Disabled fields, deselected tabs |

Hierarchy is carried by **surface lift**, not by drop shadows. There is no atmospheric gradient. There is no dark mode — Bali is daylight; the brand is daylight.

### Text

### Semantic

| Token | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `--color-success` | `#1f4d3a` | Same as primary — "verified" / "confirmed" are the same trust signal |
| `--color-warning` | `#b8851f` | Traffic-impacted itinerary segments, limited-availability notices |
| `--color-error` | `#a8341d` | Inline form-validation errors, "booking failed" banners |

### Color use quotas

A page is on-brand when:
- ~85% of pixel area is canvas + surface ladder + body text.
- ~10% is photography.
- ≤3% is Jungle Green fills (CTAs, brand mark, links).
- ≤2% is Terracotta (prices, scarcity, ratings, one editorial accent per landing page).

**If Terracotta covers more than 2%, drop something.** The accent loses meaning when it stops being scarce.

## Typography

### Two families

| Token | Family | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `--font-serif` | **Fraunces** (transitional serif) | Wordmark, hero headlines, section opener headlines, sub-section heads, card titles ≥22px |
| `--font-sans` | **Inter** (humanist sans) | Body, navigation, form labels, microcopy, prices, dates, fee line items |

Fallback stacks (from the production tokens):
- Serif: `'Fraunces', 'Iowan Old Style', 'Hoefler Text', Georgia, serif`
- Sans: `'Inter', ui-sans-serif, system-ui, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif`

### Hierarchy

- **Hero h1 (Fraunces, ~64px, weight 600)** — *"Real drivers. Real prices. No surprises."*
- **Section openers (Fraunces, ~48px, weight 500)**
- **Card titles ≥22px (Fraunces, weight 500)** — featured bundles, cultural events
- **Card titles <22px (Inter, weight 600)** — standard attraction / activity cards
- **Body (Inter, 16px, weight 400, line-height 1.55)** — default running text
- **Caption / eyebrow (Inter, 12px, weight 600, uppercase, +1.4px tracking)** — section labels

### Principles

- **Fraunces does the trust work.** A transitional serif (warm, slightly inked, not editorial-magazine-glossy) carries the brand's "I've been doing this a while" voice.
- **Inter does the clarity work.** Every fee, every date, every price renders in Inter — clean, legible, no decoration.
- **Modest weights.** Display tops at 600, body at 400, buttons at 500. Heavy weights signal trying too hard.
- **The price is loud but not gaudy.** Prices render in Inter, weight 600, in Terracotta — never in a custom display face, never with decorative underlines.

## Shapes

| Token | Value | Used on |
|---|---|---|
| `--radius-sm` | 8px | Buttons, form inputs, inline pills |
| `--radius-md` | 12px | Standard cards, modals |
| `--radius-lg` | 16px | Featured cards, hero panels, reservation card |
| (pill) | 9999px | Search bar, filter chips, scarcity badges, trust pills |
| (circle) | 9999px | Driver-photo avatars, search-icon orb, icon buttons |

### The verified mark

A small **circular Jungle Green check** sits at the bottom-right of every driver-photo avatar. Cap sizes 16px / 20px / 22px to match 56px / 80px / 96px avatars. The circle is the brand's silhouette for "verified" — never a square, never a lozenge, never a star.

### The refund-guarantee stamp

The editorial trust mark used once per page on attraction-detail and bundle-detail surfaces. A **circular outlined stamp** (~120×120px, 1.5px Jungle Green outline) holding the words *"REFUND GUARANTEED · DRIVER SHOWS UP OR FULL REFUND"* in eyebrow type along the circumference, with a small lock or shield icon centered.

## Photography

### The anti-stock rule

**No stock photography. No AI-generated imagery. No re-staged ceremonies.** Every image on Trivpass is:

- The actual location.
- The actual driver.
- The actual ceremony.

If we can't shoot it ourselves, we don't show it. Stock imagery breaks the brand's load-bearing claim — that the things we describe are real.

### Grading

Marketing-hero photography is graded warm-shifted and slightly desaturated — never glossy, never HDR. The look reads as *late-afternoon light through a window*, not as *Instagram beach edit*.

Booking-product photography (attraction cards, driver cards, cultural-event cards) ships ungraded — clean, clear, faithful to what the traveler will actually see.

### Driver portraits

- 4:5 portrait crop.
- Real face, real eye contact, no stock-grade smile.
- The circular verified mark anchors bottom-right, overlapping the photo edge by ~50%.

## Component signatures — the things that make Trivpass look like Trivpass

If someone takes a screenshot of one of these and posts it without the wordmark, you should still be able to tell it's Trivpass:

### 1. The wordmark — Fraunces serif in Jungle Green, with a Terracotta accent dot

The single most repeated brand element. Lowercase `trivpass`, Fraunces 600, slightly negative letter-spacing, in Jungle Green — with a small Terracotta circle to the right of the wordmark. The dot is part of the wordmark and is never removed.

### 2. The price block — Terracotta figure on warm canvas

Every card with a price renders the figure in Inter 600 Terracotta, prefixed *"From"*, suffixed with *"per traveler"* / *"per booking"*. Clean, no chrome.

### 3. The circular verified mark on driver avatars

Jungle Green circle with a parchment check icon. Anchors bottom-right of every driver photo.

### 4. The pill-shaped search bar

The *"Where → When → Travelers → Driver Type"* segmented bar, terminating in a circular Jungle Green orb with a magnifying-glass icon.

### 5. The reservation card

A sticky right-rail card with itemized fee rows. No marketplace chrome, no "service fee" surprises — the fee breakdown shows every line item (driver, tickets, fuel, parking, taxes, community contribution) and the total. The transparency promise is **shown**, not stated.

### 6. The eyebrow label

Small uppercase tracked-out caption above section headings: `"THE REAL BALI"` / `"HOW IT WORKS"` / `"FEE BREAKDOWN"`. Inter 12px, weight 600, +1.4px tracking, in Terracotta.

### 7. The reality-checked itinerary timeline

Day-by-day timeline with time markers (`07:00`, `12:00`) on the left and a vertical Jungle Green connector. Traffic-tight segments switch to a dashed warning rule.

## Do's & Don'ts (visual)

### Do

- Use warm off-white canvas (`#faf7f2`) as the page floor.
- Reserve Jungle Green for trust, brand, and primary action.
- Reserve Terracotta for *signal* — prices, scarcity, ratings. Never a CTA fill.
- Use Fraunces for display headlines, Inter for everything else.
- Use the circular verified mark on driver avatars.
- Itemize every fee.
- Lead every marketing band with real photography.

### Don't

- Don't ship pure black (`#000000`) or pure white (`#ffffff`). The system is warm — keep it warm.
- Don't introduce a third chromatic accent. No teal, no purple, no marigold, no coral.
- Don't recolor the wordmark. Jungle Green for the lettering, Terracotta for the dot — always.
- Don't render prices in a custom display face. Inter 600 Terracotta is the price treatment.
- Don't use Inter weight 800/900 in body copy. 600 is the maximum.
- Don't say *"Book Now"* on a CTA. Use *"Secure Your Driver & Tickets"*.
- Don't use stock or AI imagery.
- Don't add atmospheric gradients or decorative drop shadows.
- Don't pill-round rectangular CTAs.
- Don't ship a dark mode.
- Don't fabricate scarcity.
