## One page

### What we are

Trivpass is a vertically-integrated travel agency for Bali. That means we are the company you book through, the company that dispatches your driver, and the company you call when your flight is delayed and the pickup window has shifted. There is no middleman between you and the people who actually take you on your trip.

We sell four things:

1. **Tour Packages** — pre-built day trips (Ubud Sunrise Trio, Mt. Batur sunrise hike, East Bali Lempuyang) at fixed, all-in prices.
2. **Cultural Events** — curated access to real Bali ceremonies (Galungan village procession, Melasti sea-purification, Mekotek wood-pyramid ritual, Ogoh-Ogoh parade, Omed-Omedan, Pesta Kesenian Bali, Odalan Besakih) where travelers attend as invited, respectful guests of the host village.
3. **Activities & Attractions** — official tickets for water parks, wildlife parks, watersports, ATV, rafting, cultural shows, temples, and Bali's popular photo spots — with optional Trivpass driver add-on.
4. **Custom Trips** — a traveler describes their dates, pace, and vibe; our AI proposes three bespoke itineraries; the traveler books directly, or waits for a human-refined version.

All of it is bookable on a single transparent checkout. Everything you pay for is itemized — driver, tickets, fuel, parking, taxes, water — before you commit.

### What we are *not*

- **Not a marketplace.** No third-party operators are listed on Trivpass collecting a commission from the booking.
- **Not an OTA reseller.** We are not Klook, GetYourGuide, Viator, or a white-labeled version of any of them.
- **Not an aggregator.** We do not scrape inventory from other tour operators.
- **Not a hotel concierge.** We have no commission deals with hotels; we are not constrained to what a hotel's vendor list contains.
- **Not a Telegram-driver booker.** Our drivers are vetted, contracted directly, insured under our policy, and on a documented onboarding program — not freelancers picked from a WhatsApp group.

The whole positioning hinges on **vertical integration**. Trivpass *is* the agency, *is* the dispatch, *is* the support line.

### Why this exists

Bali's existing travel infrastructure is built on the broker model: tourist gets a driver, driver gets paid by tourist, driver also gets paid by every silver shop, sarong vendor, and coffee plantation he stops at along the way. Tourists arrive expecting one thing and get another — staged ceremonies, "free" lunches that aren't free, "this is on the way" detours that aren't. We built Trivpass to remove that layer entirely: drivers we vet, train, and contract ourselves so they don't need to chase tips, cultural events curated with the host village so they're real, prices stated all-in so nobody is haggling at the gate.

The model isn't software. It's the agency.

## What to say when someone asks

**"Are you like Klook?"** → "We are not a marketplace. We are an agency with our own drivers and our own cultural-event calendar — Klook resells third-party tours; we run ours ourselves."

**"Are you a startup or a tour company?"** → "We are a tour company. We use software because dispatch and booking benefit from it — but the product is the agency."

**"Is this AI travel?"** → "We have an AI custom-trip builder, yes — but the trip itself is run by our internal drivers and ops team in Seminyak. The AI helps you plan; the agency takes you on the trip."

**"Where are you based?"** → "Seminyak, Bali. The whole team and the entire driver roster operates out of Bali."
