# Trivpass Meta Execution Plan

> Operating guide for Facebook, Instagram, and Meta Ads. Built from the Trivpass brand book and intended for launch execution, not theory.

---

## 1. Strategic Position

Trivpass should enter Meta as the calm anti-OTA for Bali travel.

The core message is:

**Real drivers. Real prices. No surprises.**

Everything on Facebook, Instagram, and ads should answer one of three traveler anxieties:

| Traveler anxiety | Trivpass answer | Content angle |
|---|---|---|
| Will I get scammed? | Drivers are vetted and contracted directly by Trivpass | Driver roster, no commission stops, driver intro email |
| Am I being overcharged? | All-in pricing is itemized before checkout | Fee breakdowns, itinerary pricing, refund windows |
| Will the booking actually work? | 24/7 ops in Seminyak owns the trip | Day-before driver details, backup driver, support line |

Do not position Trivpass as a platform, marketplace, OTA reseller, luxury concierge, or discount aggregator.

## 3. Asset Rules

Use:

- `brand/logo/icon-square.png` for profile photos.
- `brand/logo/trivpass-mark.svg` for small watermarks.
- `brand/logo/trivpass-wordmark.svg` where the wordmark is readable.
- Jungle Green `#1f4d3a`, Terracotta `#c2603e`, Warm Off-White `#faf7f2`.
- Real photography of actual locations, drivers, vehicles, ceremonies, and trips.

Do not use:

- Stock travel photography.
- AI-generated Bali scenery.
- Re-staged ceremony imagery.
- Generic luxury travel templates.
- Purple, teal, navy, or glossy gradients.

If real photography is unavailable, use a branded text-led graphic on warm off-white paper texture with the mark only. The copy must carry the idea; the image should not fake the product.

## 4. Organic Content System

### Pillars

| Pillar | Ratio | Job |
|---|---:|---|
| Real Bali | 35% | Build authority through useful cultural and travel education |
| Our Trips | 35% | Turn attention into trip consideration |
| How Trivpass Works | 30% | Build trust and answer booking anxiety |

The existing 40/35/25 split is close. I would increase trust content to 30% at launch because Trivpass is new and the brand promise needs proof.

### Weekly Rhythm

| Day | Format | Pillar |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Carousel or single image | How Trivpass Works |
| Wednesday | Reel or carousel | Real Bali |
| Friday | Product post | Our Trips |
| Saturday | Reel | Real Bali or Our Trips |
| Stories | 5 days/week | Polls, FAQs, day-of details, reposts, behind the scenes |

### Caption Formula

```
[Specific hook answering one anxiety or teaching one Bali fact.]

[2-4 short sentences with numbers, times, inclusions, or practical details.]

[CTA]

[8-12 hashtags]
```

CTA examples:

- Secure Your Driver & Tickets.
- Save this before your Bali trip.
- DM us your travel dates.
- Link in bio for the full itinerary and fee breakdown.

Avoid:

- Book now.
- Authentic, hidden gem, magical, breathtaking, hassle-free, luxury, once-in-a-lifetime.
- Claims that cultural events are live before written agreements exist.

## 6. Paid Ads Roadmap

Do not start conversion ads until tracking and booking flow are ready.

### Phase 0: Foundation

Required before spend:

- Facebook Page and Instagram connected in the same Business Portfolio.
- Domain verified.
- Dataset/pixel installed.
- Conversions API planned or installed.
- Events tested: `PageView`, `ViewContent`, `Lead`, `AddToCart`, `InitiateCheckout`, `Purchase`.
- Payment method added.
- UTM naming convention ready.

### Phase 1: Warm Launch

| Setting | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Objective | Engagement or Traffic |
| Budget | USD 5-10/day |
| Audience | Australia, UK, Canada, USA; ages 28-55; Bali, Indonesia travel, family travel, cultural travel |
| Creative | Brand intro, driver vetting, transparent pricing |
| Goal | Learn which anxiety angle gets attention |

### Phase 2: Trip Consideration

| Setting | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Objective | Traffic or Leads |
| Budget | USD 10-20/day |
| Audience | Same markets plus engaged travelers and site visitors |
| Creative | Ubud, Mt. Batur, Uluwatu, Activities & Attractions |
| Goal | Drive itinerary views, DMs, and email leads |

### Phase 3: Conversion

| Setting | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Objective | Sales |
| Budget | USD 20-50/day after tracking is stable |
| Audience | Retarget site visitors, add-to-cart, IG engagers, lookalikes after volume exists |
| Creative | Product-specific ads with all-in pricing and inclusions |
| Goal | Paid bookings |

## 9. Community Management

### DM Response SLA

| Message type | Target response |
|---|---|
| Booking inquiry | Within 2 hours during WITA business day |
| Safety concern | Immediate escalation to ops |
| General travel question | Same day |
| Partnership/vendor pitch | 1-2 business days |

### DM Qualification Questions

Ask these in order:

1. What dates will you be in Bali?
2. How many travelers?
3. Where are you staying?
4. Do you want a fixed day trip, attraction tickets, or a custom plan?
5. Any must-visit places or things you want to avoid?

Then send the most relevant trip link or move the user to `hello@trivpass.com` / `trips@trivpass.com` for higher-touch planning.

### Reply Tone

Use calm, specific answers. Do not overpromise.

Example:

> Yes, we can help with Uluwatu, Kecak, and Jimbaran in one route. Standard timing is Uluwatu around 16:00, Kecak at sunset, dinner after. Send your date, hotel area, and traveler count and we will point you to the right option.

## 10. Reporting

### Weekly Organic Report

Track:

- Follower growth.
- Reach by pillar.
- Saves by post.
- Profile visits.
- Website taps.
- DMs generated.
- Top 3 comments/questions.

### Weekly Paid Report

Track:

- Spend.
- CPM.
- CTR.
- CPC.
- Landing page views.
- Leads.
- Cost per lead.
- Add to cart.
- Purchase.
- Cost per purchase.

### Decision Rules

| Signal | Action |
|---|---|
| Saves are high on Real Bali posts | Turn topic into carousel series |
| DMs are high but clicks low | Add clearer link-in-bio and Story CTA |
| CTR below 0.8% | Rewrite hook or change creative |
| CPC above USD 0.80 in Phase 1 | Narrow audience or test a stronger anxiety angle |
| Add-to-cart but no purchases | Audit checkout clarity, price shock, payment options |
| Comments ask the same question 3+ times | Make a dedicated post and Highlight slide |
