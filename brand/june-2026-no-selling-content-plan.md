# Trivpass · June 2026 No-Selling Content Plan

> Goal: make Trivpass visible, useful, and trusted before asking travelers to buy. This month is about frequency, education, and brand credibility.

---

## Month Strategy

June content should not push direct sales. The job is to make travelers think:

**"This is the Bali travel account that tells me what I actually need to know."**

### Content Mix

| Pillar | Ratio | Role |
|---|---:|---|
| Bali Travel Education | 40% | Helpful tips, etiquette, common mistakes, planning guidance |
| Trust Building | 35% | Driver vetting, transparent pricing, no commission stops, 24/7 ops |
| Brand Introduction | 25% | What Trivpass is, what it is not, why the agency model matters |

### Weekly Frequency

| Format | Frequency |
|---|---:|
| Feed / carousel | 5x per week |
| Reels | 3x per week |
| Stories | Daily |
| Facebook-native FAQ/text post | 2x per week |

### CTA Rules

Use soft CTAs only:

- Save this before your Bali trip.
- Follow Trivpass for honest Bali travel planning.
- DM us your Bali question.
- Which part of Bali are you planning first?
- Send this to someone planning Bali.

Avoid:

- Book now.
- Secure Your Driver & Tickets.
- Limited time.
- Discounts.
- Hard package selling.
- Claims that cultural events are live before agreements exist.
