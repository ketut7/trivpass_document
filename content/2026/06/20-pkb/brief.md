## Caption

Pesta Kesenian Bali (PKB) is on now — 13 June to 11 July 2026, across 16 venues in Denpasar centred on the Art Center. It's the island's 48th arts festival, and the 2026 theme is Atma Kerthi: honouring the perfected soul.

It isn't built as a tourist show — gamelan, dance, parades, craft, and exhibitions, shared by local families, students, and artists. Enjoy it slowly: check the schedule near your date, pick one or two things, dress neatly, and give performers space.

The only real friction is parking. Denpasar gets tight near the venue when programmes are on, so skip your own vehicle — arrive with a driver and set an evening pickup with a little walking time.

Save this for your June trip — or DM "PKB" and we'll help with the Denpasar drive.

## Reference — PKB programme

Source: official site https://pestakesenianbali.id/ (PKB XLVIII · 48th).

- **16 venues** across Denpasar, centred on the Art Center (Taman Budaya).
- **10 official programme categories:**
  - Wimbakara — arts & culture competitions
  - Widyatula — symposiums
  - Rekasadana — performances from across Bali
  - Kandarupa — cultural exhibitions
  - Utsawa — themed parades
  - Peed Aya — processions
  - Adhi Sewaka Nugraha — arts awards
  - Jantra Tradisi Bali — regional culture weeks
  - Bali World Culture Celebration — international cultural celebration
  - Kriyaloka — workshops
- Theme & exact dates are not shown on the homepage; dates (13 Jun – 11 Jul 2026) and theme (*Atma Kerthi: Jiwa Sidha Parisudha* — Indonesian: *Memuliakan Jiwa Paripurna*) per Trivlog. A daily schedule PDF is on the official site.
