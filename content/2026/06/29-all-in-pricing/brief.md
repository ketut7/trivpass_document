## Caption

The number you see is the number you pay. A Trivpass day trip already includes your driver, tickets, fuel, parking, and route planning — the only extras are a larger vehicle or a far-out hotel pickup zone, and both show on the receipt before you confirm.

Example — East Bali Gate of Heaven & Water Palaces: IDR 875,000 per adult, with tickets and everything on the road included. Add a bigger van or a distant-zone pickup if you need it.

Free cancellation up to 24h before pickup (inside 24h it's non-refundable). Your driver's name, contact, and plate arrive the night before. One team in Seminyak owns every trip, 24/7.
