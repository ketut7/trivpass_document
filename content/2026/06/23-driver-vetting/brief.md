## Caption

Most Bali driver scams start before the pickup. A driver from a chat group advertises themselves — no vetting, no vehicle check, no one to call when it goes sideways.

Every Trivpass driver earns their place over a 4–6 week onboarding: document and background review, a 38-point vehicle inspection, a CEFR B1 English assessment, and 30 supervised probation trips. They're internal contractors paid enough to never need a commission stop.

The night before your trip, you get the driver's name, photo, plate, phone, and languages — backed by 24/7 ops in Seminyak and a 15-minute backup-driver SLA.
